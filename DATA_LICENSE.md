## Intended use and compatibility

ASCEND and SwitchLingua are used only as source material for non-commercial
academic research on context-sensitive code-switching and multilingual
generation. We do not redistribute their original records.

The released Prompt Cards are newly constructed research materials. They do not
contain the original source records and are not intended to reconstruct,
identify, or replace those records.

The released materials are intended for research, benchmarking, and evaluation.
They should not be used for profiling individuals, making decisions about
people's language ability or identity, or treating generated narratives as
naturalistic evidence of real-world speakers.
